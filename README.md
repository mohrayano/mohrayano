### About
I'm **Rayane (Moh)** from Algeria. I build **AI automation and custom AI tools** that save business owners hours of repetitive work.

- 2+ years working with AI
- 400+ hours of prompt engineering inside AI code, using **Python**
- Heading toward becoming an **AI engineer**, learning by building real things
- I create **Arabic AI & Business content** on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube

### What I build
- **Process automation:** turning repetitive manual tasks into workflows that run on their own
- **Custom AI tools:** small tools built around a specific business case
- **Open-source templates:** automation patterns shared here to learn from

### How I work
1. **Free 15-minute audit** to find the repetitive task that costs the most time
2. **A clear proposal** for one specific automation and its expected result
3. **A pilot project** with a fixed scope, timeline and price, agreed in writing
4. **Monthly maintenance** to add more automations if the pilot helps

### Principles
Honest estimates over promises, simple over complex, data safety first, human review on important decisions, and ethical work only.
